# ICSSC Brain Games 2025 - Blindfolded Coding

## Introduction: It’s Dangerous to Code! Take This (Blindfold).

It’s National Deep Dish Pizza Day!

You, a highly trained pizza delivery specialist at Brain Games Arcade, have received a mission from your boss. Your task? Deliver a critical pizza order to a large group of overcaffeinated programmers, sleep-deprived engineers, and students who think debugging in production is a personality trait (couldn’t be you, of course).

But this isn’t just any delivery. This is **the** delivery. The one that could make or break your career, your reputation, and—let’s be real—your chances of affording groceries this week.

The market’s been tough, and this gig might just be your ticket to keeping the lights on—so don’t mess this up! With the arcade's “50 Minutes or It’s Free” policy breathing down your neck, failure is not an option. If you mess this up, you won’t just be losing out on a tip—you’ll be personally funding a feast for an entire CS club that hasn’t seen daylight since finals.

All that stands between you and a successful delivery? A journey of cheesy conundrums, saucy solutions, and exactly zero margin for error.

Your path is uncertain, your time is limited, and—for some reason—you’ll be doing this blindfolded.

No pressure.
