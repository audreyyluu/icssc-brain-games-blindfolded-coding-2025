# Problem 3. The Great Pizza Partition

ERROR: Gamers refuse to share unless they eat the same amount of pizza.

CS students are fine with spaghetti code, but when it comes to food, they demand strict allocation.

One guy says he only eats 1/8 of a pizza. Another will only eat 1/4 slices. The one wearing a “Linux > Windows” hoodie insists he needs an entire pizza to himself.

You watch as people start forming groups based on their preferred pizza fractions, eyeing each other with silent judgment. Someone suggests just cutting the pizzas however they want, but that idea is immediately shot down. “That’s not how this works,” someone mutters. Another person pulls out a napkin and starts scribbling numbers like it’s a midterm question.

Might as well do the math yourself before this turns into a full-blown debate.

# The Task
Your input will be a list of fractions, each representing the portion of a pizza an individual will eat. All fractions will be multiples of ⅛ and given in their simplest form (e.g., 6/8 will be written as 3/4, not 6/8). Individuals will only share a pizza with others who are eating the exact same portion.
- Determine the minimum number of whole pizzas needed to feed everyone. Return an integer.
<br></br>

    **Sample Input**:
    `[1/8, 1/4, 1/8, 1/2, 1/4, 1/4, 1/8]`

    **Sample Output**:
    `3`
